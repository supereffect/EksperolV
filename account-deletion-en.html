Eksperol - Account and Data Deletion Request

This page explains how Eksperol users can request account and related data deletion.

App: Eksperol

Delete your account from within the app

  1. Open the Eksperol app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. In the Account section, tap "Delete account permanently".
  4. Confirm the deletion prompt.

If you cannot access the app

You can also request deletion by email:

  • Email: segehancakir@gmail.com
  • Subject: Eksperol Account Deletion Request
  • Message: Include the email linked to your account and your request.

Data deleted

  • Firebase Authentication account record (UID, email, display name)
  • Vehicle garage data
  • Inspection history, notes, and scoring summaries
  • Active inspection session data

Data retained and retention periods

  • Technical security/audit logs may be retained for up to 30 days.
  • Records required by law may be retained for the legally required duration.

After the retention period, data is permanently deleted or anonymized.

Important

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
