Eksperol Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Data we process

When an account is created, we may process the email address, user identifier, and optional profile name. Vehicle details, license plate, checklist answers, notes, and photos selected by the user may be processed while using the app.

Purposes

Data is used to generate inspection reports, preserve history, synchronize across devices, secure accounts, and provide user support. Data is not sold for advertising.

Storage and transfers

Guest data is stored on the device. Vehicle and check data for signed-in accounts may be stored using Google Firebase. PDF exports and sharing occur through a third-party app selected by the user.

Photos and permissions

Camera and photo-library access is used only when the user chooses to attach an image to a checklist item. Other checklist features remain available when permission is denied.

Deletion and retention

Accounts and associated data can be deleted from Settings in the app. Users without access to the app can contact egehancakir@gmail.com. Unless retention is legally required, account-associated data is removed following deletion.

Your rights and contact

For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact egehancakir@gmail.com. Eksperol is a preliminary self-check tool and does not replace a professional or official vehicle inspection.
